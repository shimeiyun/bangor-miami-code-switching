THE DATA JOURNEY

From messy files to one defensible measure.

The pipeline reads CHAT headers and heterogeneous token TSVs, reconciles speaker codes with questionnaire records only when recording, age and sex uniquely agree, and preserves unresolved mappings. The event detector then counts adjacent eng → spa or spa → eng labels inside one recording, speaker and utterance. Mixed labels reset the sequence.

Education codes are researcher-defined ordinal groups, not verified qualification labels. The comparison is exploratory, not causal.

HOW TO READ THIS

Low = ordinal codes 1–2; Middle = 3; High = 4–5. These are not official educational attainment categories. The Low group has only five participants.

INTERPRETATION

Careful measurement is the contribution.

The strict mapping and event rules reduce the available sample, but protect validity. The result does not support a reliable education effect. The metric also captures only one narrow kind of within-utterance switching; conversational context and token exposure remain future modelling questions.
